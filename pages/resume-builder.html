Frequently Asked Questions

What is an ATS resume and why does it matter?

An ATS (Applicant Tracking System) resume is structured to be parsed by automated hiring software used by companies like Google, Amazon, and Infosys. It uses standard fonts, clear section headings (EXPERIENCE, EDUCATION, SKILLS), no tables or images, and keyword-rich bullet points. Without ATS optimization, even a great resume gets filtered out before a recruiter ever sees it.

Is this resume builder completely free?

Yes — 100% free, forever. No account creation, no email signup, no hidden charges. Just fill in your details and download your professional PDF resume instantly from the browser.

What's the difference between Fresher and Experienced resume?

A Fresher resume (0–1 year) focuses on education, academic projects, skills, internships and certifications since there is no full-time work history. An Experienced resume (1+ years) prominently features work experience with quantified achievements, a professional summary, and career progression. Our builder automatically adjusts the layout and prompts based on your selection.

How do I download my resume as a PDF?

After clicking "Generate ATS Resume", a live preview will appear. Click the Download PDF button — this opens your browser's print dialog. Select "Save as PDF" as the destination and click Save. Your clean, ATS-ready resume will be saved to your device.

What skills should a developer include in a resume?

For frontend: React, Angular, Vue.js, JavaScript, TypeScript, HTML, CSS. For backend: Node.js, Express, REST APIs, GraphQL, MongoDB, PostgreSQL. For full-stack: both sets. Always include tools like Git, Docker, AWS, CI/CD. Match keywords from the job description for the best ATS score.

Can I use this for non-developer roles?

Absolutely. While this builder is optimized for software developers, the ATS formatting principles apply to all roles. Any professional can use it — just customize the sections with your specific industry skills, experience and education.
